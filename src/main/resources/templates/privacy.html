Privacy and data use

Understand how this coursework prototype uses information before creating an account.

Account information

Registration stores your name, email address and a password hash. Designer registration also stores company details and contact information for administrator review. Do not submit real personal information or private client material to this demonstration.

Passwords are hashed with BCrypt. Login sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. This prototype currently prints password-reset links to application logs; those logs must be restricted to the development team.

Demonstration content

Project cards, favourites and dashboard counts currently use sample data. The displayed view and save counts are illustrative, not measurements of real visitor activity.

Planned analytics

Visitor analytics and personalised recommendations are not implemented. Before introducing optional analytics, the team plans to provide an explicit opt-in choice, an equally accessible opt-out, and a clear explanation of the data collected.

The proposed design uses minimal event data and aggregate reports, excludes passwords and contact details, sets a retention period, and reviews whether recommendations unfairly favour already popular designers.

Questions and corrections

Contact the project team through your course group channel for questions or to request correction or removal of demonstration account data. Self-service data export and deletion are not implemented.
